# Commitment Café – Card Decks

Print-ready card decks, templates and text versions for the **Commitment Café**
exercise (effectuation / entrepreneurship workshop). They accompany the chapter
"The Commitment Café": the online appendix of the chapter links to the files below.

## What is in the box

- **Commitment Voucher**
  - printable template, four vouchers per page, on which stakeholders record each commitment
- **Founder Information Card**
  - cards with the three ground rules for founding teams (present only your means, open with the question, close with a specific Ask)
- **Lemonade Intervention Cards**
  - instructor-only event cards that introduce unforeseen twists during the exercise (optional)
  - 2 versions: long and condensed/short card texts
- **Post-Work Sheet**
  - one-page handout for the post-work assignment (founders, stakeholders, and an alternative for everyone)
- **Quilt Canvas**
  - printable team canvas (A3 / Tabloid) with the five areas of the Crazy Quilt
- **Rotation Plan**
  - which team sits at which table in which round: the recommended shift for 4 to 10 teams with an example team (page 1) and the full assignment of every team in every round (page 2)
- **Stakeholder Role Cards**
  - role cards for the stakeholders represented in the workshop (public personality on the front, hidden context and potential commitment on the back)
  - 2 versions: long and condensed/short card texts

Every item comes in two versions: **metric** (A4 / A3) and **imperial** (US Letter / Tabloid).
There are two kinds of files:

1. **Print-ready PDFs** – the visually designed cards and templates (see below).
2. **Text versions (Word)** – the plain text of the same material as editable Word documents,
   one card or template per page, in A4 and US Letter. Use them to read, translate, adapt or
   print the content in your own layout.

The files are published as assets of the [releases](../../releases); this repository itself contains only this
README and the license.

## Printing the cards

Each card deck PDF is print-ready: bleed, crop and registration marks, rounded card
corners, plus a legend card and a compact card index on the first index
page (card number, colour, type and title of every card, handy for looking up a
card during the workshop).

| Deck | Metric | Imperial | Colour coding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stakeholder Role Cards | DIN A6 landscape (148.5 × 105 mm) on A4 | 4 × 6 in index card, landscape, on US Letter | one colour and icon per category (A, B, C) |
| Lemonade Intervention Cards | DIN A7 portrait (74 × 105 mm) on A4 | 3 × 5 in index card on US Letter | one colour; icon for the mode (scheduled / situational) |

- **Double-sided printing:** each sheet carries the fronts first and the matching backs
  afterwards. Print duplex; if front and back do not line up on your printer, choose the
  other flip option (long edge or short edge) in the print dialog and print a test sheet first.
- **Role cards:** print one complete set per café room. For the *café cards* (one per table), print a second set
  of the role cards on the front side only (role name and public personality). The stakeholder
  keeps the complete role card.
- **Cutting:** use the crop marks and cut along the card frame.
- **Vouchers and founder cards:** four per sheet; cut along the cutting lines.

Which cards to draw for a given number of tables, and how the exercise runs, is explained in the chapter.
